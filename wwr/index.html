Our History

Since 2018, WhiteWater has grown from a small local rafting guide service into a full-featured adventure outfitter. We began on the Rogue River and now offer guided trips on three Pacific Northwest rivers, welcoming families, first-time rafters, and seasoned paddlers with safe, eco-friendly river experiences.
