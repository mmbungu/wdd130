Our Rafting Adventures

Class IV Challenge

Experience the adrenaline of high-intensity rapids on our most popular expert-led trip. This full-day adventure covers 12 miles of the Rogue River with Class III–IV whitewater, cliffside scenery, and a riverside lunch. Best for ages 14+ with prior paddling experience.

Duration: 6 hours  |  Difficulty: Advanced

Book Now

Family Float

A scenic, relaxing journey through the canyon perfect for all ages and skill levels. Gentle Class I–II rapids, wildlife viewing, and calm swimming spots make this half-day trip ideal for families and first-time rafters.

Duration: 3 hours  |  Difficulty: Beginner

Book Now

Overnight Safari

Two days of rafting combined with riverside camping under the stars. Paddle through varied rapids by day, then enjoy a campfire dinner, stargazing, and a second morning on the water before returning to base camp.

Duration: 2 days  |  Difficulty: Intermediate

Available Equipment

WhiteWater provides all essential safety and paddling gear for every trip. You only need to bring swimwear, closed-toe shoes, and sun protection.

  • Coast Guard–approved life jackets (all sizes)
  • Helmets for high-intensity rapids
  • Professional-grade paddles
  • Inflatable rafts and safety throw bags
  • Wetsuits and splash jackets (seasonal)
  • Waterproof dry bags for personal items
  • First-aid kits and emergency communication devices
